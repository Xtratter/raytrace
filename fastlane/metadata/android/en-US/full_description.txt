Raytrace is a realtime ray tracer for Android. A scene of mirror and glass spheres, a torus, a Menger sponge and glowing lamps is rendered on the GPU with Rust and Vulkan (wgpu).

- Hybrid mode with soft shadows, global illumination, caustics, reflections and refraction, and a progressive path-tracing mode
- Temporal reprojection and an a-trous denoiser for a clean, stable image
- Adaptive resolution that holds your target fps
- Presets: Performance, Balanced, Quality
- Material 3 Expressive settings with five tabs, themes, haptics; English and Russian
- Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset the camera

Requires an arm64 device with Vulkan 1.1 (tuned for Adreno GPUs). Open source, Apache-2.0.
